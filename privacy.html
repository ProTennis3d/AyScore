2. Principle of data minimisation

Protecting your data is a top priority. The App processes personal data as sparingly as possible, following the principles of Art. 5 GDPR (lawfulness, data minimisation, purpose limitation, storage limitation). The provider operates no own servers: your match, note, photo and health data are in principle stored exclusively locally on your device. There is no tracking, no creation of usage or advertising profiles and no sharing with advertising networks. No third-party analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs and no cookies are used.

3. Categories of data processed

a) Match data: player names, scores, statistics, category, location (free text), weather, notes and optional photos entered by you. Stored locally on the device.

b) iCloud backup/sync (optional, Premium): When enabled, match data is stored in your private iCloud (Apple CloudKit) via your Apple ID account and synchronised between your devices. iCloud is operated by Apple; the provider has no access to it.

c) Health data (optional): Only with your explicit authorisation of Apple Health/Workout are heart rate, active calories and distance covered recorded per match and assigned to the match. This data remains on your device (or in your iCloud) and is not transmitted to the provider.

d) Purchases (App Store/StoreKit): The purchase of Premium is handled exclusively via Apple. The provider receives from Apple only the anonymised purchase or entitlement status, no payment or contact data.

e) Photos: only the images you explicitly select, stored locally.

f) Technical data: The App itself collects no device, usage or diagnostic data. Any crash and performance diagnostics occur only if you have enabled this towards Apple in the iOS settings; the recipient is then exclusively Apple.

There is no location tracking; a location is stored – if at all – only as freely entered text.

4. Purposes and legal bases

The processing of match data serves to provide the core function (recording and analysing your matches); the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of the user relationship) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a functional, stable app).

iCloud sync and the recording of health data take place exclusively on the basis of your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Health data are special categories of personal data; their processing is additionally based on your explicit consent pursuant to Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR. Purchase handling is based on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

5. No use for advertising or sales purposes

Your personal data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties for advertising purposes. Processing takes place exclusively for the purposes stated in this policy.

6. Recipients and processors

No data is shared with third parties, except with Apple as operator of the platform (App Store, StoreKit) and – when using the iCloud backup – as operator of iCloud/CloudKit. Insofar as Apple processes data on behalf of the provider, a processing relationship under Art. 28 GDPR exists; otherwise Apple is an independent controller and processes data under its own privacy terms. Beyond this, no data is transmitted to third parties.

7. Transfer to third countries

Insofar as you use iCloud or make purchases, Apple may also process data in countries outside the EU/EEA (in particular the USA). Apple provides appropriate safeguards within the meaning of Art. 44 et seq. GDPR (including EU standard contractual clauses and – where applicable – certification under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework). The provider itself does not transfer any data to third countries.

8. Storage period

Your data is stored until you delete it in the App, deactivate the iCloud backup or remove the iCloud data, or uninstall the App. Any consent granted (health, iCloud) can be withdrawn at any time with effect for the future. In addition, the principles of storage limitation under Art. 5(1)(e) GDPR apply.

9. Your data subject rights

Under the GDPR you have the following rights: access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection to processing (Art. 21). You can withdraw any consent granted at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR) without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out up to that point. As the data resides locally on your device, you can exercise many of these rights directly in the App or in the iOS settings (e.g. delete matches, remove app data). An informal message to the email address above is sufficient to exercise them.

10. Withdrawing health and iCloud authorisation

You can withdraw the Apple Health authorisation at any time in the iOS settings under “Privacy & Security > Health” or in the Health app. You can deactivate the iCloud backup in the App or in your device’s iCloud settings.

12. No automated decision-making

There is no automated decision-making, including profiling, within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.

13. Children

The App is not specifically directed at children. For consent in connection with information society services, the minimum age of Art. 8 GDPR applies, which member states set between 13 and 16 years (14 years in Austria). Younger persons should only use the App with the consent of their legal guardians.

14. Necessity of provision

Providing your data is neither legally nor contractually required. However, without the inputs required for use (e.g. match or player names), individual functions cannot be provided. Optional functions (health, iCloud) require your voluntary consent.

15. Data security

The provider takes appropriate technical and organisational measures under Art. 32 GDPR. In particular, the data benefits from the sandbox isolation and device encryption of iOS/iPadOS/watchOS and – in the case of iCloud – from Apple’s security measures.

16. Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be adjusted if the App, the services used or the legal situation change. The version included in the current app version applies in each case.
